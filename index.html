How to read this, and what it is not

Each coloured cell is the LHASA XGBoost probability that a rainfall-triggered landslide occurs in that ~1 km cell on a day with the slider conditions. It is a model output, not a measured frequency for that hillside, and not a flood forecast. The model saturates near 1 under extreme sliders — that is what the model says, not a claim that a landslide is certain.

Inputs, in model order: Lithology, Slope, PGA, soil moisture (gwetprof), antecedent rain, rain ÷ p99. Static layers are the 30-arcsecond (~1 km) LHASA static files, clipped to 97–106°E, 5.5–20.5°N (1080 × 1800 cells). Dynamic inputs here are scenario sliders, not live IMERG/SMAP data — a live version would still need a daily feed for those three.

Tap or click any cell to see its real terrain values, p99, rain ratio and probability. Drag to pan, pinch or use the +/− control to zoom.
